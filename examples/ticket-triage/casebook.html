Principles behind the evaluation design

These are the existing ten design principles. Category mappings are explicit authored or model-proposed rationale, not a compliance score.

Outcome over imitation

Permit equivalent correct answers and sensible organization.

How it appears: Different wording or same wording can both pass.

Preference: substantive fit outranks a golden answer.

Preserve meaning

Factual fidelity and material qualifications outrank cosmetic brevity.

How it appears: No unsupported cause, owner, completion, confidence or scope.

Invariant: do not knowingly reward a false answer.

Bounded comprehensiveness

Account for every declared target subdimension and visible omission.

How it appears: Use the coverage ledger; do not infer universal coverage from a case count.

Customer may expand scope with explicit new requirements.

Controlled contrasts

Prefer one meaningful input change with all other fields stable.

How it appears: Exact changed paths and causal limitations accompany each pair.

Some desired comparisons bundle role and purpose; disclose that limitation.

Balanced challenge

Include clear success and stronger-evidence controls alongside stress.

How it appears: Always refuse, always hedge and always follow the latest source should not win.

No severity is inferred from dramatic language alone.

Selection earns its place

Each family adds a distinct important mechanism or contrast.

How it appears: Retain/revise/replace/add decisions are documented against the original collection.

No claim that a globally exhaustive candidate pool was ranked.

Priority is not score

Separate dimension importance, failure severity and case allocation.

How it appears: Changing weights never changes the factual answer or counts as measured performance.

Recommended profile is author-reasoned and customer-editable.

Traceable authority

Expected behavior follows explicit task policy and supplied source passages.

How it appears: Requirements, synthetic world rules and inferred design choices are labelled.

High-stakes domain authority must be supplied separately.

Honest insight

State useful failure hypotheses with alternatives and disconfirmation.

How it appears: Do not label an unrun case a discovered target defect.

Scoring and actual execution are optional downstream services.

Usable ownership

Deliver complete inputs, explanations and adaptation rules.

How it appears: No private state or conversation history is required to inspect a case.

Only candidate JSONL goes to the target; workbook is operator material.

Target dimensions and subdimensions

These describe the target system. They anchor the category and theme coverage above.

  • Intent over keywords: Different user needs can mention the same business object.
  • Evidence before commitment: A route must be supported by the information available.
  • Instructions stay outside ticket data: Source-embedded requests cannot rewrite the routing policy.
The separate 11 generator-quality responsibilities

These evaluate the creation process and collection, not eleven scores to impose on every target.

  1. User and evaluation intent: Intended decision, stakes, reviewer and exclusions
  2. Agent and operating context: Actual evaluated boundary, observations, tools/state and missing access
  3. Domain grounding: Source authority, applicability, synthetic assumptions and expertise gaps
  4. Coverage and diagnostic ambition: Declared universe, depth, contrasts, interactions and omissions
  5. Scenario/interaction quality: Coherent world, information boundaries and plausible behavior
  6. Acceptable behavior: Outcome standards, alternatives, permissions and unresolved disagreement
  7. Measurement/diagnosis: Observable evidence, evaluator challenges and supported interpretations
  8. Usefulness/usability: First-use instructions, adaptation work and actionable open questions
  9. Generation competence: Reproducible stages, admission/rejection reasons and assistance log
  10. Operational dependability: Versioned delivery, honest partial states, safe effects and real resource accounting
  11. Evolution/compounding: Exposure history, revision triggers, regression controls and scoped learning

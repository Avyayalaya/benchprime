# BenchPrime: Alder project-update evaluations

Private partial preview. No target scores.

Prepare a decision-useful project update for an operations lead deciding whether a limited rollout can begin on the scheduled date.

12 test definitions across 5 populated categories and 5 scenario groups, with 133 checks.

## Material limitations

- Definition-only private preview. The customer target was not executed and no score or production-readiness claim is made.
- Designed scenarios are not sampled customer traffic; completeness is limited to the supplied task contract.
- Source authority and review labels are declared inputs. Exact hashes and separate worker contexts do not prove domain correctness or human acceptance.
- Internal illustrative answers are kept out of candidate inputs. They are authored examples, not observed target results.
- The bridge preserves source/criteria text; it does not silently repair semantic defects or certify the benchmark methods.
- Model-stage output has not been calibrated by an independent human/domain reviewer.
- Some criteria were revised with prior keys and explicit reviewer feedback. Those revision calls were not blind derivations; candidate inputs stayed fixed and prior versions are retained.
- Model-generated cases and keys with operator-guided engineering/model revisions. Five criterion wordings were corrected by the AI operator after model review and are marked proposed; they have not been independently recalibrated. Six comparison descriptions and two titles were corrected. Internal illustrative controls remain only in the raw research pack. Candidate inputs are unchanged.
